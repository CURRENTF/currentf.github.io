About Me

我是山东大学的硕士研究生，研究方向为**高效大模型**。我于2022年在山东大学获得了计算机科学学士学位（人工智能实验班）。本科期间，我曾获得 ACM/ICPC 区域赛银牌，展现了较强的代码实现能力。

I am a Master's student at Shandong University with a research focus on **efficient large-scale models**. I received my B.S. in Computer Science (AI Experimental Class) from the same university in 2022. During my undergraduate studies, I won a Silver Medal in the ACM/ICPC Regional Contest, highlighting my strong programming skills.
